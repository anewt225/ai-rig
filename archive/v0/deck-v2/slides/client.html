A franchisee with five ice cream stores wanted help with inventory, training and POS data. Their managers spent their analysis time collecting data. The same patterns apply: agree the KPIs first and which source owns each one, because the POS, the franchisor portal and the scheduling tool will disagree; put reconciliation and reorder math in code; use the model where tolerance is acceptable, like estimating how full a tub is from a photo, and for the manager-facing narrative. A manager’s confirmation is the record, and the brief proposes rather than sends. Pilot in one store, then roll out.
