Evaluation - scoring outputs

Resume: 9 weighted dimensions; composite is 40% quality, 60% fit to the posting

Cover letter: 7 dimensions; 50% quality, 50% alignment

LinkedIn: 100 points, led by discoverability (32) and relevance (26)

Interview: 1–5 against CAR, FIT and TIARA, after my own self-rating

All of this lives in one plugin: 4 skills and 13 commands, with the target role passed in as a parameter. Seven linked Notion databases hold the data: opportunities, companies, contacts, communications, interviews, tasks and sprints. Notion wins any disagreement; HubSpot is my interface only and automations never read it. Every resume or cover letter is scored before and after tailoring, so each change is measured. The debrief fetches the Fireflies transcript by ID, collects my three-question self-assessment before the model’s analysis, checks Notion for an existing interview record before creating one, and drafts the thank-you note for me to send. The capture run is currently inactive because unattended runs stall on a write approval; capture runs by hand until that is fixed.
