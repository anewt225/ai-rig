Rules I build by

  1. A forcing cadence turns logging into decisions
  2. Computation in cron, judgment in the agent
  3. Actions, not status
  4. Automate the boring ceremonies, not the judgment
  5. One owner per entity
  6. Grade every count by evidence
  7. Verify it myself; date every claim
  8. Safe to re-run; nothing deleted

Which source wins: Notion for roles, contacts and messages; TickTick for doing; Fireflies for transcripts; the ledger for money. Mirrors are for people, never for automations.

The rules come from my own notes, not a framework: the first four from an article I am drafting on running agile ceremonies for one person, the rest from the operating model I keep for the system. “Computation in cron, judgment in the agent” is the one I would pick if I could keep only one: scripts produce every number, and the model interprets and prioritizes but never fetches and transforms.
