Home and health

Kitchen inventory in Notion, wearable sleep data, and a network-loss log that got an ISP fault fixed.

Python · PowerShell · Notion

Money has to be exact, so everything up to the report is code. The pipeline parses each institution’s export format into one schema (date, account reduced to its last four digits, description, amount with money in positive and money out negative, flow type, source file), appends to a master ledger keyed by an MD5 hash of date, account, amount and description, and checks every account’s balance against a balances file before writing a reconcile report. The model only categorizes edge cases and explains variances. No institution names, account numbers or figures appear in this deck. The home-network log ran continuously from startup; after five failed technician visits the evidence got a senior crew sent, who found an unauthorized splitter, and packet loss fell from 1.05% to 0.09%.
