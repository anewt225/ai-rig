Applying it to a five-store franchise

Managers spent their analysis time collecting data. Start from the decision each KPI drives and the source that owns it.

This answers the question I get most often: what would the back end look like for a real client, here a franchisee with five stores. Same patterns as my own system. First, agree the KPIs and which source owns each one, because the POS, the franchisor portal and the scheduling tool will all disagree. Then split the work: reconciliation and reorder math in code; the model where tolerance is acceptable, like estimating how full a tub is from a photo, and for the manager-facing narrative. A manager’s confirmation is the record, and the brief proposes rather than sends. I would pilot in one store for two weeks and measure time saved on inventory against the manager’s old process.
