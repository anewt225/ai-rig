Here is the deterministic versus model trade-off made concrete. I wanted to know where my time actually goes across a laptop, three browsers and two phones. Early on the model re-parsed raw logs every week, which was slow, costly and impossible to audit. Now four Windows tasks and eight Python scripts compute everything into a 2 KB summary, and the review agent only interprets it, so the numbers in my review cannot be invented. The table is the part I would emphasize with a client: these are real failure modes, race conditions at login, stale data labeled OK, silent success on total failure, and each fix makes failure loud. Reconciling trackers also surfaced that 69 percent of my screen time was on phones, which the pipeline had been missing entirely.
