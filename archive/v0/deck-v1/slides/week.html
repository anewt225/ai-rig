Agents never call each other. Each run writes a dated file that the next run checks for.

This is the cadence. Sunday noon the deterministic jobs run. Sunday evening the review agent interprets their output. Then I step away and reflect on paper, which is the one step I deliberately did not automate. Weekdays, the sync runs at 7:07 and the brief at 8:00. The design choice worth calling out: hand-offs are files, not agent-to-agent calls. The brief checks for today’s sync file up to three times, five minutes apart, then falls back to the most recent one and labels its date. That makes failures visible and every step replayable.
