One plugin I wrote runs the whole pipeline: 4 skills and 13 commands, with the target role as a parameter, plus an event-prep skill drafted from six real events this fall. Capture pulls saved postings from Reader into Notion and dedupes by URL. Tailor scores a resume or cover letter before and after rewriting. Apply logs each application with a named person attached. Network: Events vets the ticket and the room, ranks every session, tiers the people, writes a brief and calendar blocks, then turns the debrief into follow-ups. Network: Individuals drafts outreach from the Notion CRM. Interview covers prep, mock practice and the debrief, which scores the Fireflies transcript against CAR, FIT and TIARA after I rate myself. Brand keeps the LinkedIn profile aligned to the target role.
