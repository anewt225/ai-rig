A real proposal from February 2026, client anonymized. The owner runs four ice cream stores (three open, one opening) and a fast-growing catering business, with little multi-unit support from the franchisor. Production was set each day from a visual count and a spreadsheet averaging two weeks of usage, and it didn’t sync between stores. He wanted staff to understand the why behind each number, so the batch sheet shows its math and allows overrides. Discover: shadow managers and the weekly GM meeting, map data flows, score vendors against a custom build. Design: dashboard wireframes, an iPad inventory form, the data model, a pilot plan. Deliver: a one-store pilot run in parallel for a week, then rollout, training and SOPs. In March he built his own version with AI in about two weeks. Build versus buy has shifted, and the consulting value moves to scoping, data definitions and adoption.
