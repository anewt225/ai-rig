Of Eliza’s four services, my portfolio lines up most closely with AI Enablement for knowledge work, which runs five steps: Find the right workflows (frequent, painful, context-heavy, reviewable), Set up the workspace (files, tools, connectors, instructions, permissions, guardrails), Run on real work, Build reusable skills, plug-ins or agents, and Measure usage, artifacts, time saved and quality. I did each of those for myself: three workflows chosen for frequency and reviewability, 32 connectors plus a custom MCP server with step files and guardrails, routines that have produced 127 reports, four plugins with 11 skills and 29 commands, and a scorecard built around conversations per week. The franchise proposal follows AI Deployment’s two phases, and the cost work (quiet-day exits, precomputed summaries, a smaller model for the daily sync) is AI Optimization in miniature. Evaluation is the gap: I use scoring rubrics and evidence rules, but no golden dataset or release gates yet.
