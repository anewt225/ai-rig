Same pattern as the other workflows: code does anything that must be exact, Claude interprets, and I decide. The finance pipeline is Python: it parses each institution’s export into one schema, appends to a master ledger keyed by a hash so re-runs never duplicate, and checks every account’s balance before writing a reconcile report. The tax assistant is a plugin with five skills that organizes documents, works the calculations step by step, and writes filing guidance; I file. The kitchen inventory is a Notion database of about 190 items kept current from zone photos and receipts; Claude previews every change and writes only after I approve. Travel is the panorama pipeline (2,394 photos time-corrected and geotagged, 38 raw bursts stitched with ExifTool, rawpy, OpenCV and Hugin), a trip dashboard, and the Baldwin audio walking tour. No institution names, account numbers or figures appear here.
